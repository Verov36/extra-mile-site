# Article briefs

Three articles ship in `src/content/posts.ts`. These are next, in priority order. Each
targets a question an owner actually searches, answers it fully, and links to one service
page. Aim for 500 to 800 words, plain language, no filler.

| 10 | Is it safe to give a service access to my business email? | is it safe to outsource email management | email-triage, privacy |

Rules for every article:

- The first paragraph answers the question. No warm-up.
- Include one thing the reader can do today without hiring anyone.
- Mention Extra Mile once, near the end, and only where it honestly fits.
- No invented statistics. If a number is not from the September survey or a named public
  source, leave it out.
